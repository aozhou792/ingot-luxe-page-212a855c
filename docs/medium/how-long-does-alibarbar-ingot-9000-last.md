One of the most common questions Australian buyers ask is how long a single Alibarbar Ingot 9000 will last. The honest answer: it depends on how you vape. Here's a realistic breakdown.

## Quick answer

At about **300 puffs a day**, expect roughly **3–4 weeks** from one Ingot 9000. Light use (~150/day) can stretch toward **6 weeks**; heavy use (~600/day) is closer to **2 weeks**. Longer, harder draws shorten that window.

- Rated for up to 9000 puffs — real life varies with puff length  
- Average use (~300 puffs/day): about 3–4 weeks

- Shorter puffs and cool storage extend device life  
- Use the LED display to plan your next replacement  

## The simple maths

Each device is rated for up to 9000 puffs. Divide that by your typical daily puff count for a rough estimate of how many days it will last.

| Daily use | Puffs/day | Rough lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| Light | ~150 | Around 6 weeks |
| Average | ~300 | Around 3–4 weeks |
| Heavy | ~600 | Around 2 weeks |

These are estimates — puff length and draw style matter as much as puff count.

## Make each device last longer

- Take shorter puffs — long, hard draws burn through e-liquid and battery faster  
- Store it cool and upright, away from direct heat  
- Watch the LED so you can plan a replacement before it runs dry  

## FAQ

**Is the Ingot 9000 rechargeable?** No. It arrives pre-charged and isn't designed to be recharged — replace it when the battery or e-liquid is finished.

**How do I know when it's finished?** The smart LED shows remaining battery and e-liquid, so you're not guessing.
